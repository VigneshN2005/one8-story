How to choose cricket shoes

A short guide for club and college players.

1. Spikes or rubber soles?

Spikes give the best grip on grass pitches and are what professionals use. Rubber-soled shoes are better for matting, astro turf and nets. If you play mostly on turf wickets, choose spikes.

2. Batting or bowling?

Batters need grip and quick movement, so a lighter shoe with a secure heel works best. Fast bowlers land hard on the front foot, so they need more cushioning and ankle support.

3. Fit and comfort

Try shoes on with the socks you play in. Leave a thumb's width at the toe, and make sure the heel does not slip.

What Virat Kohli's fitness routine teaches us

Kohli is known for his strict fitness routine: strength training, sprint work and a disciplined diet. Good footwear is part of that. Shoes that support your movement help you train more and get injured less.
