What is one8?

one8 is a sportswear brand co-founded by Virat Kohli. The name comes from his jersey number, 18. For years it was sold as a line with Puma, and in December 2025 it became an independent brand backed by Agilitas Sports. Today it makes cricket shoes, running and training shoes, everyday sneakers and gym wear.
