one8 collections: cricket shoes, sneakers and gym wear

An overview of what one8 sells, grouped by sport.

Cricket shoes and spikes

The Cover Drive 18 is one8's cricket shoe for batters. It has 11 spikes, a stiff shank for stability when you shift your weight, and a heel lock collar. Pro and X versions are available.

White sneakers for men and women

The Seam XVIII comes in red for Test cricket and white for the shorter formats. The Seam Classic is the cheaper everyday version, starting around Rs 4,999.

Running and training shoes

The Boom Rush is a multi-activity trainer for running and gym sessions.

Gym t-shirts and apparel

one8 also sells t-shirts, polos, jackets, shorts and caps for training and daily wear.
