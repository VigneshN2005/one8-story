About one8 by Virat Kohli

From a Puma collaboration to an independent Indian sportswear brand.

Timeline

YearWhat happened
2017one8 starts as Virat Kohli's lifestyle label and is sold as a line with Puma.
Dec 2025Kohli moves the one8 brand to Agilitas Sports and invests about Rs 40 crore in the company.
2026one8 relaunches as a performance sports brand with the Seam XVIII, Cover Drive 18 and Boom Rush, sold on one8.com, Myntra and Flipkart.

What makes one8 different

Most celebrity shoes only carry a name. one8 products are designed with Kohli and worn by him on the field. The brand also sells in limited drops, which is why some releases sell out within minutes.

Who it is for

Young Indians who play or follow cricket, go to the gym or run, and want sneakers they can wear every day.
